  This chart shows daily solar radiation in Hong Kong from January to June 2026. The light blue line represents daily radiation, which fluctuates sharply between about 1 and 7.5 kWh/m²/day, reflecting short-term weather variability. The thick orange line is the 7-day average, which smooths these fluctuations and reveals the broader trend.

  Overall, radiation follows a V-shaped pattern: it generally declines from January and reaches a minimum in late February to early March, around 2 kWh/m²/day, likely due to cloudy and rainy winter-spring conditions. It then rises steadily through spring, reaching the highest values in June, around 6.5 kWh/m²/day, consistent with stronger summer sunlight. The 7-day average clearly highlights this seasonal transition while filtering out day-to-day noise.
